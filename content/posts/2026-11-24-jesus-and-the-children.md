We all know how e be to be told say we too small to matter. Hello to evribodi wey dey read for Nigeria — I glad say you dey here.

People dey bring small children to Jesus for am to touch dem, but di disciples rebuke dem. When Jesus see dis, im vex. Im talk to dem, "Make di small children come to me, and make una no hinder dem, for di kingdom of God belong to such as dis. Truly I tell you, anybody wey no go receive di kingdom of God like small pikin go never enter am."

Den im take di children for im arms, put im hands on dem bless dem.

Im no tell di children make dem grow up first. Im no tell dem make dem understand first. Im take dem as dem be — and talk say di kingdom belong to people like dem.

Why di disciples dey turn di children away? Wetin e mean to receive di kingdom of God like small pikin? And who we dey keep away from Jesus today?

If you dey read dis for Nigeria — or anywhere for di world where you read Pidgin — abeg, you fit take small time greet Jonathan?
