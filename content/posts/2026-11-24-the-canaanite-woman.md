We all know how e be to keep ask when nobody seem to dey listen. Hello to evribodi wey dey read for Nigeria — I glad say you dey here.

Jesus go to di coasts of Tyre and Sidon. One woman of Canaan come out cry, "Get mercy on me, O Lord, thou Son of David; my daughter dey grievously vex with devil."

But Jesus no answer her one word. Im disciples talk, "Send her away; for she dey cry after us."

Jesus talk, "I no send but unto di lost sheep of di house of Israel."

Den she come worship am, talk say, "Lord, help me." Im answer, "E no meet to take di children bread, and to cast am to dogs."

She talk, "Truth, Lord: yet di dogs chop of di crumbs wey fall from dia masters table."

Den Jesus talk to her, "O woman, great na thy faith: make e be unto thee even as thou wilt." And her daughter don make whole from dat very hour.

*A note on di word "dogs": some don talk say Jesus dey go back and forth for dis conversation, dey enjoy to talk for light-hearted manner. E hard to understand di context of di word dem use here, so di reader suppose consider di context — and say Jesus approve of her faith and encourage her, dey press her to keep going.*

Why you think say di woman no give up? Wetin you make of Jesus answer about di crumbs? And wetin e talk say im praise di faith of stranger?

If you dey read dis for Nigeria — or anywhere for di world where you read Pidgin — abeg, you fit take small time greet Jonathan?
