We all know how e be to want sort tins out right now — to pull up wetin no belong. Hello to evribodi wey dey read for Nigeria — I glad say you dey here.

Jesus talk say di kingdom of heaven be like man wey sow good seed for im field. But while im men dey sleep, im enemy come sow weeds among di wheat go away.

When di plants come up bear grain, den di weeds appear too. Di servants come to di master talk say, "Master, you no sow good seed for your field? How den e get weeds?"

Im talk, "One enemy don do dis."

So di servants talk, "Den you want make we go gather dem?" But im talk, "No, make una no gather di weeds make una root up di wheat along with dem. Make both grow togeda until di harvest, and for harvest time I go tell di reapers, Gather di weeds first bind dem for bundles to burn, but gather di wheat inside my barn."

Why you think say im talk make we wait? Wetin go happen if dem pull up di weeds now? And wetin e mean say di harvest na di time for sorting?

If you dey read dis for Nigeria — or anywhere for di world where you read Pidgin — abeg, you fit take small time greet Jonathan?
