We all know how e be when pesin ask us to leave everitin behind. Hello to evribodi wey dey read for Nigeria — I glad say you dey here.

Jesus dey walk by di Sea of Galilee. Im see two brodas, Simon dem call Peter and Andrew im broda, dey cast net inside di sea. Dem be fishermen.

Im talk to dem, "Follow me, and I go make you fishers of men." And dem leave dia nets at once follow am.

Small further on im see two more brodas, James and John, di sons of Zebedee. Dem dey for boat with dia papa, dey mend dia nets. Im call dem too. And dem leave di boat and dia papa, follow am.

Wetin you for do if Jesus call you dat day? Wetin go hardest for you to leave behind? And wetin e talk say im choose fishermen first?

If you dey read dis for Nigeria — or anywhere for di world where you read Pidgin — abeg, you fit take small time greet Jonathan?

*Di Bible words for here na dem translate am for dis site.*
