*Di Unjust Judge and di Importunate Widow, after John Everett Millais, engraved by di Brothers Dalziel (1863–64), Metropolitan Museum of Art, public domain.*

We all know how e be to be ignored when we ask for help. Hello to evribodi wey dey read for Nigeria — I glad say you dey here.

Jesus tell dis story so im disciples go always pray and no give up. E get judge for one town wey neither fear God nor care wetin people think. And e get widow for dat town wey keep come to am talk say, "Give me justice against my adversary."

For long time di judge refuse. But at last im talk to imsef, "Even though I no fear God or care wetin people think, I go see say she get justice, because dis widow dey keep bother me."

Den Jesus talk, "Listen to wetin di unjust judge talk. And God no go bring justice for im chosen ones, wey dey cry out to am day and night? Im go see say dem get justice, and quick."

Wetin you dey keep ask God for? Wetin e go mean to keep ask and no give up? And wetin Jesus question mean — when im come, im go find faith for earth?

If you dey read dis for Nigeria — or anywhere for di world where you read Pidgin — abeg, you fit take small time greet Jonathan?
