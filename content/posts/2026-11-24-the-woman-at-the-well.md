We all know how e be to thirst for sometin more. Hello to evribodi wey dey read for Nigeria — I glad say you dey here.

Jesus, tired from di journey, sit down by well. E be about noon. One Samaritan woman come draw water, and Jesus talk to her, "You go give me drink?"

"You be Jew and I be Samaritan woman," she talk. "How you fit ask me for drink?"

Jesus answer, "If you know di gift of God, you for ask am and im for give you living water. Everibodi wey drink dis water go thirsty again, but whoever drink di water I give dem go never thirst. E go become inside dem spring of water wey dey well up to eternal life."

"Sir, give me dis water," she talk.

Im tell her, "Go, call your husband come back."

"I get no husband," she reply.

"You right," Jesus talk. "You don get five husbands, and di man you get now no be your husband."

"Sir, I fit see say you be prophet," she talk. "I know say Messiah dey come. When im come, im go explain everitin to us."

Den Jesus declare, "I, di one wey dey talk to you — na me."

Di woman leave her water jar go back to di town. "Come, see one man wey tell me everitin I ever do," she talk. "Dis fit be di Messiah?" Many Samaritans believe for am because of her testimony. Im stay two days, and many more believe because of im own word. "Now we don hear for ourselves," dem talk, "and we know say dis man really na di Saviour of di world."

Wetin you dey thirst for? Wetin e go mean to never thirst again? And who you go tell, if you meet am for di well?

If you dey read dis for Nigeria — or anywhere for di world where you read Pidgin — abeg, you fit take small time greet Jonathan?

*Di Bible words for here na dem translate am for dis site.*
