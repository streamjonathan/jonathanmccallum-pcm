For di night before im die, Jesus pray out loud, with Im friends dey listen. Im pray for dem — and for all who go believe.

And for di middle of am im talk sometin strange: "Father, glorify me for your presence with di glory I get with you before di world begin."

Before di world begin. Before anytin at all dey, im dey there, with di Father, dey share Im glory. Den im come here, to us. And now im dey ask to go home.

Im no claim glory im go earn one day. Im talk of glory im already get, before time.

Wetin im be like, before di world begin? Why im go leave dat glory come here? And wetin e mean say im pray for you?
