*Di Miracle of di Loaves and di Fishes, Bartolomé Esteban Murillo (1617–1682), public domain.*

Di day before, Jesus don feed five thousand people with five small bread and two fish. Di next morning di crowd come look for am again. Dem don cross di lake to find am. Dem want more bread.

Jesus tell dem say, "No work for di food wey dey spoil. Work for di food wey dey last."

Dem ask am, "Wetin we must do to do di works wey God require?"

Jesus answer, "Di work of God na dis: to believe for di one wey Im don send."

Den Im talk say, "I am di bread of life. Anybody wey come to me no go ever hungry again."

Im no offer dem more bread. Im offer dem Imsef.

Wetin you dey hungry for? Wetin e go mean to come to am? And wetin dey stop you?

*Di Bible words for here na dem translate am for dis site.*
