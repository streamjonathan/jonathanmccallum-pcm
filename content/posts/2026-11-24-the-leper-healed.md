We all know how e be to be kept for distance. Hello to evribodi wey dey read for Nigeria — I glad say you dey here.

One man with leprosy come to Jesus beg am on im knees, "If you willing, you fit make me clean."

Jesus move with compassion. Im reach out im hand touch di man. "I willing," im talk. "Be clean!" Immediately di leprosy leave am and im cleanse.

Jesus send am away at once with strong warning: "See say you no tell dis to anybody. But go, show yourself to di priest offer di sacrifices wey Moses command for your cleansing, as testimony to dem."

Instead im go out begin talk freely, dey spread di news. As result, Jesus no fit enter town openly again but stay outside for lonely places. Yet di people still come to am from everywhere.

Why di leper talk "if you willing" — and no be "if you able"? Wetin e mean say Jesus touch am before im heal am? And wetin you go talk to Jesus if you kneel before am like dat?

If you dey read dis for Nigeria — or anywhere for di world where you read Pidgin — abeg, you fit take small time greet Jonathan?

*Di Bible words for here na dem translate am for dis site.*
