We all know how e be when dem pass you by and you need help. Hello to evribodi wey dey read for Nigeria — I glad say you dey here.

One man dey go down from Jerusalem to Jericho, robbers attack am. Dem strip am, beat am, leave am half dead. One priest come by, see am, pass by for di oda side. One Levite do di same.

Den one Samaritan come. Im see di man, and im get pity for am. Im tie im wounds, pour oil and wine, put am for im own animal, carry am go inn. Di next day im give di innkeeper two denarii talk say, "Take care of am. Any extra money you spend, I go pay you when I come back."

"Wich of dis three na neighbour?" Jesus ask. "Di one wey show am mercy." "Go and do di same," Jesus talk.

Who na di neighbour you least expect? When dem don pass you by — or you don pass odas by? And wetin e go cost you to stop and help?

If you dey read dis for Nigeria — or anywhere for di world where you read Pidgin — abeg, you fit take small time greet Jonathan?

*Di Bible words for here na dem translate am for dis site.*
