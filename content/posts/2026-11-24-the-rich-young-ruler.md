We all know how e be to get almost everitin — and still want more. Hello to evribodi wey dey read for Nigeria — I glad say you dey here.

One man run up to Jesus fall on im knees. "Good teacher," im ask, "wetin I must do to inherit eternal life?"

"Why you call me good?" Jesus reply. "Nobody good — except God alone. You know di commandments: 'You shall not murder, you shall not commit adultery, you shall not steal, you shall not give false testimony, you shall not defraud, honour your papa and mama.'"

"Teacher," im talk, "all dis I don keep since I be boy."

Jesus look at am love am. "One tin you lack," im talk. "Go, sell everitin you get give to di poor, and you go get treasure for heaven. Den come, follow me."

For dis di man face fall. Im go away sad, because im get great wealth.

Jesus talk, "How hard e be for di rich to enter di kingdom of God! E easy for camel to go through eye of needle pass for pesin wey rich to enter di kingdom of God."

Di disciples amaze. "Who den fit save?" Jesus talk, "With man dis impossible, but no be with God; all tins possible with God."

Peter talk, "We don leave everitin follow you!" "You go receive hundred times as much," Jesus talk, "and eternal life."

Wetin na di one tin you go find hardest to give up? Why Jesus love di man and still let am walk away? And wetin e mean say wetin impossible for us na possible with God?

If you dey read dis for Nigeria — or anywhere for di world where you read Pidgin — abeg, you fit take small time greet Jonathan?
