"I am di true vine," Jesus talk, "and my Father na di gardener." Im talk dis to Im friends for di night before im die.

Branch no fit bear fruit by imsef. E must stay join to di vine. Cut off, e go wither.

"I am di vine; una be di branches," im talk. "If una remain for me and I for una, una go bear much fruit; apart from me una fit do notin."

Di gardener prune di branches wey dey bear fruit, so dem go bear even more.

Im no talk say try harder. Im talk say stay connected.

Wetin you join to? Wetin fruit dey grow for your life? And wetin e go mean to stay for am?

*Di Bible words for here na dem translate am for dis site.*
