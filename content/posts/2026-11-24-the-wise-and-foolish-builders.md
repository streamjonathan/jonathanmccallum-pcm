Storms dey come to every house. Di question na wetin di house build on. Hello to evribodi wey dey read for Nigeria — I glad say you dey here.

"Everibodi wey hear my words and do dem," Jesus talk, "na like wise man wey build im house on rock. Rain fall, floods come, winds blow beat dat house — and e no fall, because dem build am on rock."

"But everibodi wey hear my words and no do dem na like foolish man wey build im house on sand. Rain fall, floods come, winds blow beat dat house — and e fall. And great na di fall of am."

Wetin you dey build your life on? Wich of Jesus words you dey hear but you no dey do? And wetin e go look like to build on di rock?

If you dey read dis for Nigeria — or anywhere for di world where you read Pidgin — abeg, you fit take small time greet Jonathan?

*Di Bible words for here na dem translate am for dis site.*
