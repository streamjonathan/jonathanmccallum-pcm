*Di Raising of Jairus Daughter, Ilya Repin (1871), public domain.*

We all know how e be when hope run out. Hello to evribodi wey dey read for Nigeria — I glad say you dey here.

Jairus, one of di synagogue leaders, come fall for Jesus feet, dey plead earnestly with am. "My small daughter dey die," im talk. "Abeg come put your hands on her so say she go heal live." So Jesus go with am.

For di way, some people come from Jairus house. "Your daughter don die," dem talk. "Why bother di teacher again?"

Jesus hear wetin dem talk, tell Jairus, "Make you no fear; just believe." Im no let anybody follow am except Peter, James and John.

When dem come to di house, Jesus see commotion, with people dey cry wail loudly. "Why all dis commotion and wailing?" im ask. "Di pikin no die but sleep." But dem laugh at am.

Im put dem all out, take di pikin papa and mama and di disciples wey dey with am. Im take her by di hand talk to her, "Talitha koum!" — wey mean, "Small girl, I talk to you, get up!"

Immediately di girl, wey be twelve years old, stand up begin walk around. Dem completely astonish. Im give strict orders make nobody know about dis, tell dem make dem give her sometin to chop.

Wetin you for do when di messengers talk say she don die? Why Jesus talk say she dey sleep? And wetin "make you no fear, just believe" mean for you today?

If you dey read dis for Nigeria — or anywhere for di world where you read Pidgin — abeg, you fit take small time greet Jonathan?

*Di Bible words for here na dem translate am for dis site.*
