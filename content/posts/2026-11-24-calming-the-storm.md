We all know how e be when everitin around us dey fall apart. Hello to evribodi wey dey read for Nigeria — I glad say you dey here.

Dat day when evening come, Jesus talk to im disciples, "Make we go over to di oda side." Leave di crowd behind, dem take am along, just as im be, for di boat. Furious squall come up, and di waves break over di boat, so say e nearly sink.

Jesus dey for stern, dey sleep on cushion. Di disciples wake am talk to am, "Teacher, you no care if we drown?"

Im get up, rebuke di wind talk to di waves, "Quiet! Be still!" Den di wind die down and e completely calm.

Im talk to im disciples, "Why you so fear? You still get no faith?"

Dem fear and ask each oda, "Who be dis? Even di wind and di waves obey am!"

Wetin storm you dey inside right now? You think say im care? And who be dis, say even di wind and di waves obey am?

If you dey read dis for Nigeria — or anywhere for di world where you read Pidgin — abeg, you fit take small time greet Jonathan?
