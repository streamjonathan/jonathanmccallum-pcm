*Di Parable of di Talents, Willem de Poorter, public domain.*

We all don receive sometin — and di question na wetin we do with am. Hello to evribodi wey dey read for Nigeria — I glad say you dey here.

One man wey wan travel call im servants give dem im money: five talents to one, two to anoda, one to anoda — each according to im ability.

Di first trade make five more. Di second make two more. But di third dig hole hide im talent for ground.

When di master return, im talk to di first two, "Well done, good and faithful servant." But di third talk, "I fear, so I hide your talent for ground." Di master answer, "You wicked and lazy servant. You for put my money with di bankers." Im take di talent away give am to di one wey get ten. Di servant wey hide im talent dem throw am out inside darkness.

"To everibodi wey get," Jesus talk, "dem go give am more."

Wetin dem don give you? You dey use am, or you dey bury am? And wetin e go look like to hear "well done"?

If you dey read dis for Nigeria — or anywhere for di world where you read Pidgin — abeg, you fit take small time greet Jonathan?

*Di Bible words for here na dem translate am for dis site.*
