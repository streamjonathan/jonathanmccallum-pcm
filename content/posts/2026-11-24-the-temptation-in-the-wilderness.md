We all know how e be to hungry, tired — and tempted to take shortcut. Hello to evribodi wey dey read for Nigeria — I glad say you dey here.

Spirit lead Jesus go wilderness to be tempted by di devil. After fasting forty days and forty nights, im hungry. Di tempter come to am talk say, "If you be di Son of God, tell dis stones to become bread."

Jesus answer, "E dey written: 'Man no go live on bread alone, but on every word wey come from di mouth of God.'"

Den di devil take am to di holy city make am stand for di highest point of di temple. "If you be di Son of God," im talk, "throw yourself down. For e dey written: 'Im go command im angels concerning you, and dem go lift you up for dia hands, so say you no go strike your foot against stone.'"

Jesus answer am, "E dey also written: 'Make you no put di Lord your God to test.'"

Again, di devil take am to very high mountain show am all di kingdoms of di world and dia splendour. "All dis I go give you," im talk, "if you go bow down worship me."

Jesus talk to am, "Comot from me, Satan! For e dey written: 'Worship di Lord your God, and serve am only.'" Den di devil leave am, and angels come attend am.

Wetin di three temptations get for common? Why Jesus answer each one with scripture? And wetin shortcut you dey tempted to take today?

If you dey read dis for Nigeria — or anywhere for di world where you read Pidgin — abeg, you fit take small time greet Jonathan?
