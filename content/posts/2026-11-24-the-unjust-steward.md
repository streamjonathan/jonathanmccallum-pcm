We all know how e be to worry about money. Hello to evribodi wey dey read for Nigeria — I glad say you dey here.

Jesus tell im disciples dis story. E get rich man wey get manager dey look after im money, and dem accuse di manager say im dey waste am. So di rich man call am in talk say, "Wetin be dis I hear about you? Give account of your management, because you no fit be manager again."

Di manager talk to imsef, "Wetin I go do? I no strong enough to dig, and I shame to beg." So im get idea. Im call each pesin wey owe im master money. To one wey owe hundred measures of oil im talk, "Take your bill write fifty." To anoda wey owe hundred measures of wheat im talk, "Write eighty."

And di master commend di dishonest manager, because im don act shrewdly.

Den Jesus talk, "Nobody fit serve two masters. You no fit serve both God and money."

Wetin you make of dis strange story? Wetin e mean to use wetin you get wisely? And wich master you dey serve?

If you dey read dis for Nigeria — or anywhere for di world where you read Pidgin — abeg, you fit take small time greet Jonathan?
