*Di Parable of di Unforgiving Servant, Claude Vignon, public domain.*

To forgive person wey hurt you na one of di hardest tins. Hello to evribodi wey dey read for Nigeria — I glad say you dey here.

Peter ask, "Lord, how many times I suppose forgive? Seven times?" Jesus talk, "No be seven times, but seventy-seven times."

Den im tell story. One king wan settle accounts. One servant owe am ten thousand talents — debt im no fit ever pay. Di servant fall for im knees beg, and di king forgive am di whole debt.

But dat servant go out find fellow servant wey owe am hundred denarii. Im hold am talk say, "Pay wetin you owe!" Di man beg, but im no listen, and im throw am for prison.

When di king hear, im vex. "I forgive you all dat debt," im talk. "You no suppose get mercy, as I get mercy on you?"

Wetin debt of yours dem don forgive? Who you find hardest to forgive? And wetin go change if you forgive dem di way dem don forgive you?

If you dey read dis for Nigeria — or anywhere for di world where you read Pidgin — abeg, you fit take small time greet Jonathan?
