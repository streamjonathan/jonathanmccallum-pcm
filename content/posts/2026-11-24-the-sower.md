Some tins we hear dey enter one ear come out for di oda ear. Hello to evribodi wey dey read for Nigeria — I glad say you dey here.

One sower go out go sow. As im dey scatter di seed, some fall for road, and birds come chop am. Some fall for rocky ground, where soil no plenty. E grow quick, but when sun rise e dry, because e get no root. Some fall among thorns, and di thorns grow up choke am. But some fall for good soil, and e grow come give grain — hundred, sixty, thirty times wetin dem sow.

"Anybody wey get ear," Jesus talk, "make im hear."

Wetin kind ground you be right now? Wetin dey choke di seed for your life? And wetin good soil go look like for you?

If you dey read dis for Nigeria — or anywhere for di world where you read Pidgin — abeg, you fit take small time greet Jonathan?

*Di Bible words for here na dem translate am for dis site.*
