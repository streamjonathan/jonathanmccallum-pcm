We all know how e be to be thanked — and how e be to be forgotten. Hello to evribodi wey dey read for Nigeria — I glad say you dey here.

As Jesus dey go Jerusalem, im enter village, and ten men wey be lepers stand far off. Dem lift up dia voices talk say, "Jesus, Master, get mercy on us."

Im talk to dem, "Go show yourselves unto di priests." And as dem go, dem cleanse.

One of dem, when im see say im don heal, turn back. Im glorify God with loud voice fall down on im face for Jesus feet, dey give am thanks. And im be Samaritan.

Jesus talk, "No be ten dem cleanse? but where be di nine? Dem no find say dem return to give glory to God, save dis stranger." And im talk to am, "Arise, go thy way: thy faith don make thee whole."

Why you think say only one come back? Wetin e talk say di one wey return na Samaritan? And when last you thankful like dat?

If you dey read dis for Nigeria — or anywhere for di world where you read Pidgin — abeg, you fit take small time greet Jonathan?
