We all know how e be to no know wetin to talk when we pray. Hello to evribodi wey dey read for Nigeria — I glad say you dey here.

Jesus dey teach im disciples for di mountain. Im tell dem make dem no pray with many words to be heard. God already know wetin we need before we ask.

So im give dem dis words to pray: "Our Papa wey dey for heaven, Hallowed be thy name. Thy kingdom come. Thy will be done for earth, as e be for heaven. Give us dis day our daily bread. And forgive us our debts, as we forgive our debtors. And lead us no go inside temptation, but deliver us from evil: for thine na di kingdom, and di power, and di glory, for ever. Amen."

Na di whole prayer be dat: bread, forgiveness, and help.

Wetin you notice about how short dis prayer be? Wich line of am you need most today? And wetin go change if you pray am like you mean am?

If you dey read dis for Nigeria — or anywhere for di world where you read Pidgin — abeg, you fit take small time greet Jonathan?
