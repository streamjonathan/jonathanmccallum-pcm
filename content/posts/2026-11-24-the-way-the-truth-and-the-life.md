Jesus dey tell Im friends say im dey go away, to prepare place for dem. Thomas talk say, "Lord, we no know where you dey go, so how we go know di way?"

Jesus answer, "I am di way and di truth and di life. Nobody dey come to di Father except through me."

Im no talk say im know di way. Im talk say im be di way. No be map, no be set of directions — person.

Im no point to di road. Im talk say im be di road.

Wetin road you dey on? Wetin e go mean if di way to God na person? And where you think im dey take you go?
