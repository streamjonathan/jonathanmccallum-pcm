We all know how e be to be judged by room full of people. Hello to evribodi wey dey read for Nigeria — I glad say you dey here.

One of di Pharisees, name Simon, invite Jesus to chop with am. Jesus go in sit down.

Den one woman for di city, wey be sinner, come in with alabaster box of ointment. She stand for im feet behind am, dey cry, wash im feet with her tears. She wipe dem with di hairs of her head, kiss dem, anoint dem with di ointment.

Simon think to imsef, if dis man be prophet, im for know wetin kind woman dis be.

Jesus tell am of two men wey owe money, one five hundred pence and one fifty. Neither fit pay, and both dem forgive. "Wich of dem go love am most?" "Di one dem forgive most," Simon talk. Jesus talk say im don judge rightly.

Den Jesus talk to di woman, "Thy sins dem don forgive." And to her, "Thy faith don save thee; go for peace."

Why you think say di woman come to Jesus at all? Wetin Simon see wey Jesus no see? And wetin e mean to be forgiven like dat?

If you dey read dis for Nigeria — or anywhere for di world where you read Pidgin — abeg, you fit take small time greet Jonathan?
