*Di Parable of di Workers for Di Vineyard, Marten van Valckenborch, public domain.*

We all know how e be when sometin no look fair. Hello to evribodi wey dey read for Nigeria — I glad say you dey here.

Jesus talk say di kingdom of heaven be like master of house wey go out early for morning hire labourers for im vineyard. Im agree with dem for one denarius a day, send dem in.

Im go out again for nine for morning, for noon, and for three for afternoon, and each time im find more men stand idle send dem inside vineyard too, talk say, "Wetin dey right I go give you."

For five for afternoon im go out find odas stand idle. Im talk, "Why you stand here idle all day?" Dem talk, "Because nobody hire us." Im talk, "You go inside vineyard too."

When evening come, di owner talk to im foreman, "Call di labourers pay dem dia wages, begin with di last, up to di first." Those dem hire for five receive one denarius each. When di first come, dem think say dem go receive more. But dem too receive one denarius each.

Dem grumble. "Dis last work only one hour, and you don make dem equal to us wey don bear di burden of di day and di scorching heat."

Di master talk, "Friend, I no dey do you wrong. You no agree with me for one denarius? Take wetin be yours and go. I choose to give to dis last worker as I give to you. I no fit do wetin I choose with wetin be mine? Or you dey envy my generosity?"

Jesus finish, "So di last go be first, and di first last."

E sound fair to you? When you don feel like di worker dem hire first? And wetin e talk about God say im generous like dis?

If you dey read dis for Nigeria — or anywhere for di world where you read Pidgin — abeg, you fit take small time greet Jonathan?
