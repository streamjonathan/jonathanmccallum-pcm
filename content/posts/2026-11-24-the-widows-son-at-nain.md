*Di Raising of di Widow Son for Nain, Jan Verhas (19th century), public domain.*

We all know how e be to lose pesin we love. Hello to evribodi wey dey read for Nigeria — I glad say you dey here.

Jesus go to city dem call Nain, with im disciples and great crowd. For di gate, dem dey carry out dead man — di only son of im mama, and she be widow. Large crowd dey with her.

When Jesus see her, im get compassion on her. Im talk to her, "Make you no cry."

Den im come touch di bier. Those wey dey carry am stand still. And im talk, "Young man, I talk to you, Arise."

Di dead man sit up begin talk. And Jesus give am back to im mama.

Fear come on all of dem, and dem glorify God, talk say, "Great prophet don rise up among us," and, "God don visit im people." And dis news go out through all Judea and di country around.

Wetin you think say di widow feel when Jesus talk, "Make you no cry"? Why you think say Jesus stop di funeral? And wetin e talk about am say im give di son back to im mama?

If you dey read dis for Nigeria — or anywhere for di world where you read Pidgin — abeg, you fit take small time greet Jonathan?

*Di Bible words for here na dem translate am for dis site.*
