We all know how e be to wonder whether di small tins we do really matter. Hello to evribodi wey dey read for Nigeria — I glad say you dey here.

Jesus talk, "When di Son of Man come for im glory, and all di angels with am, den im go sit on im glorious throne. Before am dem go gather all di nations, and im go separate people one from anoda as shepherd separate di sheep from di goats. And im go put di sheep for im right, but di goats for di left."

Den di King go talk to those for im right, "Come, you wey my Papa bless, inherit di kingdom dem prepare for you from di foundation of di world. For I hungry and you give me food, I thirsty and you give me drink, I be stranger and you welcome me, I naked and you clothe me, I sick and you visit me, I for prison and you come to me."

Den di righteous go answer, "Lord, when we see you hungry feed you, or thirsty give you drink? And when we see you stranger welcome you, or naked clothe you? And when we see you sick or for prison visit you?"

And di King go answer dem, "Truly, I talk to you, as you do am to one of di least of dis my brodas, you do am to me."

Den im go talk to those for im left, "Comot from me, you cursed, inside di eternal fire dem prepare for di devil and im angels. For I hungry and you give me no food, I thirsty and you give me no drink, I stranger and you no welcome me, naked and you no clothe me, sick and for prison and you no visit me."

Dem too go ask, "Lord, when we see you like dat, and no minister to you?" And im go answer, "Truly, I talk to you, as you no do am to one of di least of dis, you no do am to me."

Jesus finish, "And dis go go away inside eternal punishment, but di righteous inside eternal life."

Who be di least of dis around you? When you don help pesin without to think twice? And wetin e mean say Jesus take am personal?

If you dey read dis for Nigeria — or anywhere for di world where you read Pidgin — abeg, you fit take small time greet Jonathan?
