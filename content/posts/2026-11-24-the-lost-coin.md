To lose small tin fit turn whole house upside down. Hello to evribodi wey dey read for Nigeria — I glad say you dey here.

"If one woman get ten silver coins and lose one," Jesus ask, "she no go light lamp, sweep di house, and search well until she find am?"

And when she find am, she call her friends and neighbours talk say, "Rejoice with me! I don find di coin wey I lose."

Jesus talk say joy dey before di angels of God over one sinner wey repent.

Wetin you go sweep whole house to find? Wetin e mean to be searched for like dat? And who you go tell first, if you find wetin lost?

If you dey read dis for Nigeria — or anywhere for di world where you read Pidgin — abeg, you fit take small time greet Jonathan?

*Di Bible words for here na dem translate am for dis site.*
