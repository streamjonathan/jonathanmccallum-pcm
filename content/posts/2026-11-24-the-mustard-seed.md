Small beginnings fit look like notin at all. Hello to evribodi wey dey read for Nigeria — I glad say you dey here.

"Di kingdom of heaven be like mustard seed," Jesus talk. E na di smallest of all seeds. One man take am sow am for im field. But when e grow, e big pass all di garden plants. E become tree — so big say birds of di air come make dia nests for im branches.

Na so God kingdom be. E start small pass wetin you fit see. And e grow become sometin wey give shelter to odas.

Wetin small tin for your life fit grow big pass wetin you expect? Where you don see sometin small become shelter for odas? And wetin e go mean say God kingdom start dis small?

If you dey read dis for Nigeria — or anywhere for di world where you read Pidgin — abeg, you fit take small time greet Jonathan?
