We all know how e be to be left out. Hello to evribodi wey dey read for Nigeria — I glad say you dey here.

Jesus go up inside mountain. Im sit down, and im disciples come to am. Den im open im mouth teach dem.

"Blessed na di poor for spirit: for theirs na di kingdom of heaven. Blessed na dem wey mourn: for dem go comfort. Blessed na di meek: for dem go inherit di earth."

Im go on. Blessed na those wey hunger and thirst for wetin right, di merciful, di pure for heart, and di peacemakers. Blessed na those wey dem persecute for doing wetin right. "Rejoice, and be exceeding glad: for great na your reward for heaven."

Di world call di rich and strong blessed. Jesus begin with di poor, di sad, and di gentle.

Wich of dis blessings surprise you most? Wetin e go mean to be poor for spirit? And wetin Jesus talk say belong to people like dat?

If you dey read dis for Nigeria — or anywhere for di world where you read Pidgin — abeg, you fit take small time greet Jonathan?

*Di Bible words for here na dem translate am for dis site.*
