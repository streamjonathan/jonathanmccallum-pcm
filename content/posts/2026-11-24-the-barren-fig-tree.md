We all know how e be to be given one more chance. Hello to evribodi wey dey read for Nigeria — I glad say you dey here.

Jesus tell dis story. One man get fig tree dey grow for im vineyard, and im go look for fruit on am but no find any. So im talk to di man wey take care of di vineyard, "For three years now I don dey come look for fruit on dis fig tree and I no find any. Cut am down! Why im go use up di soil?"

"Sir," di man reply, "leave am alone for one more year, and I go dig around am fertilize am. If e bear fruit next year, fine! If not, den cut am down."

Three years of notin. And still, one more year.

Wetin fruit your life dey bear? Wetin e go mean to be given one more year? And wetin you go do with am?

If you dey read dis for Nigeria — or anywhere for di world where you read Pidgin — abeg, you fit take small time greet Jonathan?
