Jesus talk say, "I am di good shepherd. Di good shepherd lay down Im life for di sheep."

Hired man dey run when wolf come. Di sheep no be im own, so why im go stay? But di shepherd stay. Im know Im sheep, and Im sheep know Im voice. Dem follow am because dem know di sound.

"I know my sheep," Jesus talk, "and my sheep know me."

Im no talk say im dey watch di sheep from far. Im talk say im know dem — and im go die for dem.

Whose voice you dey follow? Wetin e go mean to be known like dat? And wetin e talk say im go lay down Im life for di sheep?
