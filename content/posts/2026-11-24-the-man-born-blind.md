We all know how e be to be asked to explain sometin we no fit explain. Hello to evribodi wey dey read for Nigeria — I glad say you dey here.

Jesus pass by one man wey blind from im birth. Im disciples ask, "Master, who sin, dis man, or im parents, say dem born am blind?" Jesus talk, "Neither dis man sin, nor im parents: but say di works of God suppose make manifest for am."

Den im spit for ground, make clay with di spittle, anoint di eyes of di blind man. Im talk to am, "Go, wash for di pool of Siloam." Im go, wash, come dey see.

Di Pharisees question am too, talk say Jesus no fit be from God, because im don heal for di Sabbath. Di man talk, "One tin I know, say, whereas I blind, now I see."

Dem cast am out. Jesus find am talk say, "You believe on di Son of God?" Im talk, "Lord, I believe." And im worship am.

Why you think say di disciples ask whose fault di blindness be? Wetin you go talk if pesin ask you wetin Jesus don do for you? And wetin di man answer — "I blind, and now I see" — talk to you?

If you dey read dis for Nigeria — or anywhere for di world where you read Pidgin — abeg, you fit take small time greet Jonathan?
