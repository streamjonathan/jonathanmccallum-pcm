Philip talk to Jesus say, "Lord, show us di Father, and dat go be enough for us."

Jesus answer, "You no know me, Philip, even after I don dey among una for so long time? Anybody wey don see me don see di Father."

Im don live with dem, chop with dem, walk with dem for three years. And still dem ask to see God.

Jesus talk: look at me. Wetin I do, di Father dey do. Wetin I talk, di Father dey talk.

Im no tell dem to look somewhere else. Im tell dem to look at am.

Wetin you think God be like? If you want know, where you go look? And wetin you don see for Jesus so far?
