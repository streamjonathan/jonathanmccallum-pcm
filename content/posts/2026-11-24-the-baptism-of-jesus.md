We all know how e be to hear pesin talk who we really be. Hello to evribodi wey dey read for Nigeria — I glad say you dey here.

Jesus come from Galilee to Jordan to be baptise by John. But John try stop am, talk say, "I need to be baptise by you, and you dey come to me?"

Jesus reply, "Make e be so now; e proper for us to do dis to fulfil all righteousness." Den John agree.

As soon as dem baptise Jesus, im go up out of di water. For dat moment heaven open, and im see di Spirit of God dey come down like dove dey land on am. And voice from heaven talk, "Dis na my Son, wey I love; with am I well please."

Im don do notin yet — no miracles, no teaching, no crowds. And before any of am, di voice talk who im be: beloved, and pleasing to God.

Why Jesus go baptise, when John dey baptise people for repentance? Wetin di voice from heaven talk about am? And wetin e go mean to hear God talk say im please with you?

If you dey read dis for Nigeria — or anywhere for di world where you read Pidgin — abeg, you fit take small time greet Jonathan?

*Di Bible words for here na dem translate am for dis site.*
