My name na Jonathan. Jesus na di person wey don change my life, and di story of Aslan for The Chronicles of Narnia mean plenty to me, because na my first time to know di nature of God. I no grow up for religious family, and I thank God for how story give me space to understand God, and to thirst for more.

Aslan, especially for The Last Battle, get special power for how I see God and di world and eternity. E get one scene where Lucy and Digory and Tirian face hard decision, how dem go fight to di end with brave heart against dia enemies.

E get stable for di story. Inside dat stable e get sometin much more. For those wey get bad mind, wetin dey inside di stable na dia worst nightmare. For those wey get desire to know God, wetin happen for dat place deep well well.

Lucy talk dis, after she notice say wetin dey inside di stable big pass wetin dem see from outside. For our world too, stable once get sometin inside am wey big pass di whole world. E get sometin about Jesus wey big pass our whole world, big pass our life, big pass our universe, and yet dem born am for stable.

Imagine yourself for dat battle, where Lucy find di joy of her heart inside wetin oda people see as terrible, blind situation. Dem no fit see di big eternity inside dat stable. I hope you no miss am.
