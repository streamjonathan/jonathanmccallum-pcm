We all know how e be to get question we almost fear to ask. Hello to evribodi wey dey read for Nigeria — I glad say you dey here.

E get man name Nicodemus, Pharisee and ruler of di Jews. Im come to Jesus by night talk say, "Rabbi, we know say you be teacher come from God."

Jesus answer, "Verily, verily, I talk to you, Except person be born from above, im no fit see di kingdom of God."

Nicodemus talk, "How person fit born when im old?" Jesus talk say person must be born of water and of di Spirit. Di wind blow where e wish. You hear am, but you no fit tell where e come from. So e be with everibodi born of di Spirit.

Den Jesus talk, "For God so love di world, say im give im only begotten Son, say whosoever believe for am no go perish, but get everlasting life."

Why you think say Nicodemus come for night? Wetin you think say e mean to be born from above? And wetin e talk say God love di world dis much?

If you dey read dis for Nigeria — or anywhere for di world where you read Pidgin — abeg, you fit take small time greet Jonathan?
