To lost na lonely feeling — and to know say dem dey look for you dey change everitin. Hello to evribodi wey dey read for Nigeria — I glad say you dey here.

Jesus ask, "If one of you get hundred sheep and lose one, wetin you go do? You no go leave di ninety-nine for open country go after di lost one until you find am?"

And when im find am, im put am for im shoulders carry am go house, dey rejoice. Im call im friends and neighbours talk say, "Rejoice with me! I don find my sheep wey lost."

Jesus talk say joy dey for heaven over one sinner wey repent pass over ninety-nine wey no need repentance.

You don ever feel like di one sheep? Wetin e talk say im go look for you, instead of to wait? And who for your life fit need to know say dem dey look for dem?

If you dey read dis for Nigeria — or anywhere for di world where you read Pidgin — abeg, you fit take small time greet Jonathan?
