We all know how e be when pesin we love must go away. Hello to evribodi wey dey read for Nigeria — I glad say you dey here.

For di Last Supper, Jesus tell im friends say im dey go away. Den im talk, "And I go ask di Papa, and im go give you anoda Helper, to dey with you forever, even di Spirit of truth, wey di world no fit receive, because e neither see am nor know am. You know am, for im dey with you and go dey inside you."

"But di Helper, di Holy Spirit, wey di Papa go send for my name, im go teach you all tins and bring to your remembrance all wetin I don talk to you."

"But when di Helper come, wey I go send to you from di Papa, di Spirit of truth, wey proceed from di Papa, im go bear witness about me."

"Nevertheless, I tell you di truth: e good for you say I go away, for if I no go away, di Helper no go come to you. But if I go, I go send am to you."

"When di Spirit of truth come, im go guide you inside all di truth. Im go glorify me, for im go take wetin be mine declare am to you."

Di Son ask. Di Papa give. Di Spirit come — and stay.

Wetin e mean to get "anoda Helper"? How e feel to know say di Spirit dey with you for ever? And wetin im don remind you of wey Jesus talk?

If you dey read dis for Nigeria — or anywhere for di world where you read Pidgin — abeg, you fit take small time greet Jonathan?
