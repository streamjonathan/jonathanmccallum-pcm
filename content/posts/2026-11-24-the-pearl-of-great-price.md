We all know how e be to search for sometin — and den, at last, to find am. Hello to evribodi wey dey read for Nigeria — I glad say you dey here.

Jesus talk, "Again, di kingdom of heaven be like merchant wey dey search for fine pearls, wey, on finding one pearl of great value, go sell all wetin im get buy am."

Dis man know pearls. Im don spend im life dey look for di fine ones. Im know wetin worth sometin and wetin no worth.

Den one day im find am. One pearl. Pearl of great value. And im do wetin di man with di treasure do: im go sell all wetin im get, and im buy am.

Im no keep im oda pearls too. Im give dem all up for di one.

Wetin you dey search for? You don ever find sometin worth to give everitin up for? And wetin e go mean if di kingdom of heaven na dat one pearl?

If you dey read dis for Nigeria — or anywhere for di world where you read Pidgin — abeg, you fit take small time greet Jonathan?
