How long we fit talk about di mystery of free will? God dey above all, know all, fit do all, and somehow e dey allow tins to happen wey we no wan make happen. Di more I go along for dis video, di more I see di layers and di depth inside am.

I wan start with dis. Good people dey suffer. People dey suffer, and I no know why. Maybe you get complex answer or simple one. My hope be say we, as people wey dey follow Jesus, no go quick give answers wey go hurt person wey dey inside suffering. I think we only learn dat truth when we suffer ourself.

Terrible tins dey happen to people of all belief, all age, all culture, all place and all time. Dat one important to talk before we talk about free will.

![Jonathan dey talk about free will and Jesus](/images/free-will-and-jesus.jpg)

Forgive me to make am personal. I start to follow Jesus because I get nightmares where I feel fear well well, and I call out, Jesus, please help me. I feel deep freedom. I hope to talk am for hundreds of videos. Wetin matter na to tell my story and think about wetin God don dey do for my life.

I no dey talk say people wey dey follow Jesus no go suffer. God dey hold us, and we dey waka too and get responsibility. Deep mystery dey there, and grace inside am. I no think say even di people wey study God pass fit talk say dem don understand dat mystery of freedom, or how God fit know and be all, and hold all time and space for im hand.

So my thought be dis. Each day you wake up, you get chance to waka for dat love and dat freedom you get for Christ. Jesus don set us free from di powers wey wan control us, and from sin and death.
