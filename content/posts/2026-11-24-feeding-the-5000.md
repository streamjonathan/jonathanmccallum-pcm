We all know how e be when e no enough to go round. Hello to evribodi wey dey read for Nigeria — I glad say you dey here.

Large crowd follow Jesus, because dem see di signs im don perform by healing di sick. Jesus go up mountainside with im disciples. Seeing di great crowd dey come towards am, im talk to Philip, "Where we go buy bread for dis people to chop?"

"E go take more pass half year wages to buy enough bread for each one to get bite!" Philip answer.

Andrew talk up: "Here na boy with five small barley loaves and two small fish, but how far dem go go among so many?"

Jesus talk, "Make di people sit down." E get about five thousand men. Jesus take di loaves, give thanks, give dem to those wey sit down, as much as dem want. Im do di same with di fish.

When dem all don get enough, im talk, "Gather di pieces wey remain. Make notin waste." Dem fill twelve baskets with di pieces wey remain.

After di people see dis sign, dem talk, "Surely dis na di Prophet wey go come inside di world."

Wetin small tin you get to offer? Wetin e go mean to give am to Jesus? And wetin e talk say notin waste?

If you dey read dis for Nigeria — or anywhere for di world where you read Pidgin — abeg, you fit take small time greet Jonathan?

*Di Bible words for here na dem translate am for dis site.*
