We all know how e be to be given sometin good to look after — and to forget say e never be ours. Hello to evribodi wey dey read for Nigeria — I glad say you dey here.

Jesus talk, "E get master of house wey plant vineyard put fence around am dig winepress inside am build tower lease am to tenants, and go inside anoda country."

When di season for fruit draw near, im send im servants to di tenants to get im fruit. Di tenants take im servants beat one, kill anoda, stone anoda. Im send oda servants, more pass di first, and dem do di same to dem.

Finally im send im son, talk say, "Dem go respect my son." But when di tenants see di son, dem talk, "Dis na di heir. Come, make we kill am have im inheritance." And dem take am throw am out of di vineyard kill am.

Jesus ask, "When therefore di owner of di vineyard come, wetin im go do to those tenants?" Dem talk, "Im go put those wretches to miserable death let out di vineyard to oda tenants wey go give am di fruits for dia seasons."

Jesus talk, "You never read for di Scriptures: 'Di stone wey di builders reject don become di cornerstone; dis na di Lord doing, and e marvellous for our eyes'? Therefore I tell you, di kingdom of God dem go take away from you give to people wey dey produce im fruits. And di one wey fall on dis stone dem go break am to pieces; and when e fall on anybody, e go crush am."

Di chief priests and di Pharisees hear im parables perceive say im dey talk about dem. Dem seek to arrest am, but dem fear di crowds, because di crowds hold am to be prophet.

Wetin dem don give you to look after? How we dey treat wetin belong to pesin? And wetin e mean say di rejected stone become di cornerstone?

If you dey read dis for Nigeria — or anywhere for di world where you read Pidgin — abeg, you fit take small time greet Jonathan?
