We all know how e be to ask for help for pesin we love. Hello to evribodi wey dey read for Nigeria — I glad say you dey here.

When Jesus enter Capernaum, one centurion come to am, dey ask for help. "Lord," im talk, "my servant lie for house paralysed, dey suffer terribly."

Jesus talk, "I go come heal am?"

Di centurion reply, "Lord, I no deserve make you come under my roof. But just talk di word, and my servant go heal. For I myself be man under authority, with soldiers under me. I tell dis one, 'Go,' and im go; and dat one, 'Come,' and im come."

When Jesus hear dis, im amaze. "Truly I tell you, I no find anybody for Israel with such great faith," im talk. "Many go come from di east and di west, and go take dia places for di feast with Abraham, Isaac and Jacob for di kingdom of heaven."

Den Jesus talk to di centurion, "Go! Make e be done just as you believe am go be." And im servant heal for dat moment.

Wetin di centurion understand about authority? Why Jesus amaze? And wetin "just talk di word" go look like for your life?

If you dey read dis for Nigeria — or anywhere for di world where you read Pidgin — abeg, you fit take small time greet Jonathan?
