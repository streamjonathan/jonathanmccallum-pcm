Jesus talk say, "I am di door of di sheep."

For dat time sheepfold get only one opening. For night di shepherd Imsef go lie down for di doorway — im be di door. Thieves climb enter some oda way. But di sheep dem come in through di door.

"Anybody wey enter through me go dey saved," Jesus talk. "Dem go come in and go out, and find pasture."

Im no talk say di way in na rule, or ritual, or test. Im talk say di way in na im.

Wetin you dey look for way to enter? Wetin e mean say di way in na person, no be rule? And you don ever try to climb enter anoda way?

*Di Bible words for here na dem translate am for dis site.*
