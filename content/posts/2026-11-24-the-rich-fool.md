*Di Parable of di Rich Fool, Rembrandt van Rijn (1627), public domain.*

We all know how e be to want small more security. Hello to evribodi wey dey read for Nigeria — I glad say you dey here.

One man for di crowd talk to Jesus, "Teacher, tell my broda make im divide di inheritance with me." Jesus talk, "Man, who make me judge or divider over you?" Den im talk to dem all, "Take care, and guide against all covetousness, for person life no be di plenty of im possessions."

Den im tell dem story. Di land of one rich man produce plenty. Im think, "Wetin I go do, for I get nowhere to store my crops?" So im talk, "I go tear down my barns build bigger ones, and there I go store all my grain and my goods. And I go tell my soul, Soul, you get plenty goods lay up for many years; relax, chop, drink, make merry."

But God talk to am, "Fool! Dis night dem go ask for your soul, and di tins you don prepare, whose dem go be?"

Jesus finish, "So na di one wey lay up treasure for imsef and no rich toward God."

Wetin you dey store up? If tonight na your last night, wetin go matter most? And wetin e mean to be rich toward God?

If you dey read dis for Nigeria — or anywhere for di world where you read Pidgin — abeg, you fit take small time greet Jonathan?
