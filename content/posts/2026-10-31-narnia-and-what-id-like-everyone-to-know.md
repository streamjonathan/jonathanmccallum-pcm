When I look back to my childhood, those books na di key to my faith journey, to follow Jesus, to know who Jesus be.

![Jonathan dey talk about Narnia for green field](/images/narnia-what-id-like-everyone-to-know.jpg)

E get magic inside to know God through story, and after dat to read who e really be for di scriptures, for Jesus. People wey no too like how Lewis show God as Aslan no too like to talk to me about God, whether dem dey inside Christianity or outside. Dat one talk plenty. Lewis touch me so much say I wan know who Jesus be after I read The Chronicles of Narnia.

Aslan good reach level wey e no possible to show am for way wey go explain finish wetin Lewis dey try talk. Lewis try im best to explain who God be.

Narnia no be allegory. Na to imagine anoda kind of world — world where Jesus na lion, world wey never fall, where you fit meet di first Adam and Eve. I enjoy di BBC version well well. Di French horn dey catch my mind, my heart, anytime I hear am. If you never read Lewis, try The Great Divorce, about heaven, and The Space Trilogy — di first two books sweet well well.
