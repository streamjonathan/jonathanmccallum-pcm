We all know how e be to be told make you keep quiet. Hello to evribodi wey dey read for Nigeria — I glad say you dey here.

As Jesus and im disciples dey leave Jericho, large crowd dey follow, Bartimaeus dey sit by roadside dey beg. Im blind, son of Timaeus. When im hear say na Jesus of Nazareth, im begin shout, "Jesus, Son of David, get mercy on me!"

Many rebuke am tell am make im keep quiet, but im shout more, "Son of David, get mercy on me!"

Jesus stop talk say, "Call am."

So dem call di blind man, "Cheer up! For your feet! Im dey call you." Throw im cloak aside, im jump for im feet come to Jesus.

"Wetin you want make I do for you?" Jesus ask am.

Di blind man talk, "Rabbi, I want to see."

"Go," Jesus talk, "your faith don heal you." Immediately im receive im sight follow Jesus along di road.

Wetin you dey shout for? Who dey tell you make you keep quiet? And wetin you go ask Jesus for, if im stop ask you?

If you dey read dis for Nigeria — or anywhere for di world where you read Pidgin — abeg, you fit take small time greet Jonathan?
