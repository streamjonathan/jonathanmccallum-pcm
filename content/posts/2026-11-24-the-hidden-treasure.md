We all know how e be to find sometin so good you go give up anytin for am. Hello to evribodi wey dey read for Nigeria — I glad say you dey here.

Jesus talk, "Di kingdom of heaven be like treasure hide for field, wey one man find cover up again. Den for im joy im go sell all wetin im get buy dat field."

Na di whole story be dat. One man find treasure for field. Im hide am again. Im so full of joy say im go sell everitin wey im get — everitin — so im fit buy dat field.

Im no hesitate. Im no bargain. Im no keep small back just in case. Di treasure worth am all.

Wetin you go give everitin for? Wetin don ever make you dat joyful? And wetin e talk say Jesus compare di kingdom of heaven to treasure like dat?

If you dey read dis for Nigeria — or anywhere for di world where you read Pidgin — abeg, you fit take small time greet Jonathan?
