*Di Woman Taken for Adultery, Rembrandt van Rijn (1644), public domain.*

We all know how e be to be caught — and to expect di worst. Hello to evribodi wey dey read for Nigeria — I glad say you dey here.

Early for morning Jesus dey teach for temple courts, and di teachers of di law and di Pharisees bring one woman dem catch for adultery. Dem make her stand before di group talk to Jesus, "Teacher, dem catch dis woman for di act of adultery. For di Law Moses command us to stone such women. Now wetin you talk?" Dem dey use dis question as trap.

But Jesus bend down start to write for ground with im finger. When dem keep question am, im straighten up talk say, "Make anyone of you wey get no sin be di first to throw stone at her." Again im stoop down write for ground.

For dis, those wey hear begin go away one at a time, di older ones first, until only Jesus remain, with di woman still stand there.

"Woman, where dem be?" Jesus ask. "Nobody don condemn you?" "Nobody, sir," she talk. "Den I no condemn you too," Jesus declare. "Go now leave your life of sin."

Wetin you for do, stand for dat crowd? Why dem leave one by one? And wetin e mean say Jesus no condemn her — and still tell her make she leave her life of sin?

If you dey read dis for Nigeria — or anywhere for di world where you read Pidgin — abeg, you fit take small time greet Jonathan?
