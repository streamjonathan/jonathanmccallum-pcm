We all know how e be to compare ourself with pesin — and feel better. Hello to evribodi wey dey read for Nigeria — I glad say you dey here.

Jesus tell dis story to some wey think say dem righteous and look down on odas.

Two men go up to di temple go pray. One na Pharisee, di oda na tax collector.

Di Pharisee stand pray, "God, I thank you say I no be like oda men — thieves, unjust, adulterers — or even like dis tax collector. I fast twice a week. I give tenth of all I get."

But di tax collector stand far off. Im no go even lift im eyes to heaven. Im beat im chest talk say, "God, get mercy on me, sinner."

Jesus talk say di tax collector go house justified, no be di Pharisee. "Everibodi wey exalt imsef go humble," im talk, "but di one wey humble imsef go exalt."

Wich of di two men you see for yourself? Wetin you go pray if nobody dey listen? And wetin e mean to go house justified?

If you dey read dis for Nigeria — or anywhere for di world where you read Pidgin — abeg, you fit take small time greet Jonathan?
