John start Im gospel with claim, no be birth story. "For di beginning na di Word, and di Word dey with God, and di Word na God."

Di Word make everitin. Notin dem make without am.

Den John talk di strangest tin of all: "Di Word don become flesh and dwell among us."

God no stay far away. Im come live here, among us. We see Im glory — full of grace and truth. John di Baptist point to am and talk say, "Behold di Lamb of God."

Im no send message. Im come Imsef.

Wetin e mean say God come live among us? If Jesus na God for flesh, wetin dat talk about God? And wetin e go mean to receive am?

*Di Bible words for here na dem translate am for dis site.*
