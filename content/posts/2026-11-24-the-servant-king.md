*Christ Washing di Feet of Im Disciples, Jacopo Tintoretto (1548–1549), public domain.*

Na di night before im die. Jesus know say Im hour don come. For supper, im get up, comot Im outer robe, wrap towel round Im waist, and pour water inside basin. Den im begin wash Im disciples feet — di work of servant.

Peter talk say, "Lord, you go wash my feet?"

Jesus talk say, "You no realise now wetin I dey do, but later you go understand."

When im finish, im talk say, "Una call me Teacher and Lord, and rightly so, for na wetin I be. Now wey I, una Lord and Teacher, don wash una feet, una too suppose wash one anoda feet."

Di King wash feet. And im tell Im friends to do di same.

Wetin kind of king dey wash feet? If im be Lord and im serve like dis, wetin dat talk about greatness? And whose feet you go wash?
