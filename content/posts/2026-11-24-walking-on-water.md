We all know how e be to fear for dark. Hello to evribodi wey dey read for Nigeria — I glad say you dey here.

Jesus make im disciples enter boat go ahead to di oda side, while im dismiss di crowd. Den im go up mountainside by imsef go pray. When evening come, im dey there alone, and di boat don already far from land, waves dey beat am because wind against am.

Shortly before dawn Jesus go out to dem, dey walk on di lake. When di disciples see am, dem fear. "Na ghost," dem talk, cry out for fear.

But Jesus talk, "Take courage! Na me. Make you no fear."

"Lord, if na you," Peter reply, "tell me make I come to you on di water."

"Come," im talk.

Peter come down out of di boat walk on di water towards Jesus. But when im see di wind, im fear and, begin to sink, cry out, "Lord, save me!"

Immediately Jesus reach out im hand catch am. "You of small faith," im talk, "why you doubt?"

When dem climb inside di boat, di wind die down. Den those for di boat worship am, talk say, "Truly you be di Son of God."

Wetin you dey fear right now? Wetin e go mean to hear am talk, "Make you no fear"? And wetin you go do if im talk, "Come"?

If you dey read dis for Nigeria — or anywhere for di world where you read Pidgin — abeg, you fit take small time greet Jonathan?
