Belief for God's love. Wetin dat one look like? I wan talk about how we answer God's love. Wetin be our part, wetin be our responsibility, to receive am?

Over di years I don discover say e be actually step of forward movement toward faith, toward sense of trust. As I step out with dat small faith, e get gentleness and warmth and encouragement wey I dey feel. E dey change how I see myself and how I see oda people.

![Jonathan dey talk about how to feel God's love](/images/experiencing-gods-love.jpg)

Many times di weakness wey we get fit turn to strength. Look some of di people wey dey follow Jesus close well well, for dia love and worship of am, and dia love for oda people. Dem no be people wey society rate high. Dem get freedom and dem change. No be without failure, and ups and downs and bumps for di road. Look Peter. Look di people wey follow Jesus, both men and women. Dia background dey sweet to see, and how God's love dey change us.

So na di question I get for myself today. I believe say God love me? I dey step forward inside am, whether I feel like am or not? And wetin be some of di steps I fit take?

Before I give some ideas about ways to spend time with God, I think e important to talk say dis na opportunity wey we get today. Di light fit dey shine enter your room right now. To dey close to God fit mean to think about im love, or to think about scripture or song. E fit also mean to call your friend and talk to dem. E get many different ways to encourage ourself and oda people.

I think people dey forget many times how important e be to focus on to encourage oda people, and how much dat one dey encourage us for our waka with Jesus. God's love dey here for dis moment.
