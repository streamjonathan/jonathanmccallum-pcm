We all know how e be when sometin run out for di worst moment. Hello to evribodi wey dey read for Nigeria — I glad say you dey here.

E get marriage for Cana of Galilee, and di mama of Jesus dey there. Jesus and im disciples dem invite too.

When di wine run out, im mama talk to am, "Dem get no wine." Jesus talk to her, "Woman, wetin I get to do with thee? My hour never come."

Im mama talk to di servants, "Wetinsoever im talk to you, do am."

Six waterpots of stone stand there, for washing. Jesus talk, "Fill di waterpots with water." Dem fill dem to di brim. "Draw out now, and bear unto di governor of di feast." And dem do.

When di governor taste di water wey dem make wine, im talk to di bridegroom, "Every man for di beginning dey set forth good wine; and when men don well drink, den dat wey worse: but thou don keep di good wine until now."

Dis na di first miracle Jesus do, and im disciples believe on am.

Why you think say Jesus help for wedding? Wetin you make of im mama dey tell di servants make dem do whatever im talk? And wetin dis first miracle talk about am?

If you dey read dis for Nigeria — or anywhere for di world where you read Pidgin — abeg, you fit take small time greet Jonathan?
