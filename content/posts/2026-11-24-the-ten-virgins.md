We all know how e be to be caught unprepared. Hello to evribodi wey dey read for Nigeria — I glad say you dey here.

Jesus talk say di kingdom of heaven go be like ten virgins wey take dia lamps go meet di bridegroom. Five of dem na foolish, and five na wise. Di foolish take dia lamps but take no oil with dem. Di wise take flasks of oil with dia lamps.

Di bridegroom delay, and dem all become drowsy sleep. But for midnight cry come, "Here na di bridegroom! Come out meet am."

Den all those virgins rise trim dia lamps. Di foolish talk to di wise, "Give us some of your oil, for our lamps dey go out." But di wise answer, "Since e no go enough for us and for you, go rather to di dealers buy for yourselves."

While dem dey go buy, di bridegroom come. Those wey ready go in with am to di marriage feast, and di door shut. Afterward di oda virgins come too, talk say, "Lord, lord, open to us." But im answer, "Truly, I talk to you, I no know you."

Jesus finish, "Watch therefore, for you know neither di day nor di hour."

Wetin e mean to be ready? Wetin you no go want dem catch you without? And how you dey stay ready when di wait long?

If you dey read dis for Nigeria — or anywhere for di world where you read Pidgin — abeg, you fit take small time greet Jonathan?
