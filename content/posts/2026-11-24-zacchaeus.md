We all know how e be to be too small to see. Hello to evribodi wey dey read for Nigeria — I glad say you dey here.

Jesus enter Jericho dey pass through. One man dey there by name Zacchaeus. Im na chief tax collector and wealthy. Im wan see who Jesus be, but because im short im no fit see over di crowd. So im run ahead climb sycamore-fig tree to see am, since Jesus dey come dat way.

When Jesus reach di spot, im look up talk say, "Zacchaeus, come down immediately. I must stay for your house today." So im come down at once welcome am gladly.

All di people see dis begin mutter, "Im don go be guest of sinner."

But Zacchaeus stand up talk say, "Look, Lord! Here and now I give half of my possessions to di poor, and if I don cheat anybody out of anytin, I go pay back four times di amount."

Jesus talk to am, "Today salvation don come to dis house, because dis man too na son of Abraham. For di Son of Man come to seek and to save di lost."

Wetin lengths you go go, just to see Jesus? Wetin you go do if im call you by name? And wetin go change if im come to your house today?

If you dey read dis for Nigeria — or anywhere for di world where you read Pidgin — abeg, you fit take small time greet Jonathan?
