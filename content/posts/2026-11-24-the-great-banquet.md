We all know how e be to be invited somewhere — and how e be to say no. Hello to evribodi wey dey read for Nigeria — I glad say you dey here.

One of those wey dey chop with Jesus talk, "Blessed na everibodi wey go chop bread for di kingdom of God!" So Jesus tell dem story.

One man give great banquet invite many. When time reach, im send im servant talk say, "Come, for everitin don ready."

But dem all alike begin make excuses. Di first talk, "I don buy field, and I must go see am. Abeg excuse me." Anoda talk, "I don buy five yoke of oxen, and I go examine dem. Abeg excuse me." And anoda talk, "I don marry wife, so I no fit come."

Di servant tell im master, and di master vex. Im talk, "Go out quick to di streets and lanes of di city, bring in di poor and crippled and blind and lame." Di servant do am, talk say, "Sir, wetin you command don done, and still room dey."

So di master talk, "Go out to di highways and hedges compel people to come in, make my house full. For I tell you, none of those men wey dem invite go taste my banquet."

Wetin excuses we dey make? Who we no go ever expect to see for di table? And wetin e talk say di house full anyway?

If you dey read dis for Nigeria — or anywhere for di world where you read Pidgin — abeg, you fit take small time greet Jonathan?
