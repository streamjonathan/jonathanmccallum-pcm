*Healing of di Woman with di Issue of Blood, T'oros Roslin (13th century), public domain.*

We all know how e be to don try everitin — and notin work. Hello to evribodi wey dey read for Nigeria — I glad say you dey here.

One woman don dey subject to bleeding for twelve years. She don suffer plenty under care of many doctors spend all she get, yet instead of to get better she grow worse.

When she hear about Jesus, she come up behind am for di crowd touch im cloak, because she think, "If I just touch im clothes, I go heal." Immediately her bleeding stop and she feel for her body say she free from her suffering.

At once Jesus realise say power don go out from am. Im turn around for di crowd ask, "Who touch my clothes?" Im disciples talk, "You see di people dey crowd against you, and yet you fit ask, 'Who touch me?'"

But Jesus keep look around to see who don do am. Den di woman, know wetin don happen to her, come fall for im feet, dey tremble with fear, tell am di whole truth.

Im talk to her, "Daughter, your faith don heal you. Go for peace be free from your suffering."

Why she only dare touch im cloak? Why Jesus stop ask who touch am? And wetin you go tell am, if im ask you for di whole truth?

If you dey read dis for Nigeria — or anywhere for di world where you read Pidgin — abeg, you fit take small time greet Jonathan?
