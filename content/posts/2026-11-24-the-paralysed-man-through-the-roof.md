We all know how e be to need help we no fit get on our own. Hello to evribodi wey dey read for Nigeria — I glad say you dey here.

Jesus dey for house for Capernaum, and so many people gather say no room remain, not even outside di door. Four men come carry paralysed man on mat, but dem no fit get through di crowd. So dem dig through di roof above Jesus lower di man down on im mat.

When Jesus see dia faith, im talk to di paralysed man, "Son, your sins dem don forgive."

Some teachers of di law dey sit there, dey think, "Who fit forgive sins but God alone?"

Jesus talk, "Wich one easy: to talk to dis paralysed man, 'Your sins dem don forgive,' or to talk, 'Get up, take your mat walk'? But I want make you know say di Son of Man get authority for earth to forgive sins." Den im talk to di man, "I tell you, get up, take your mat go house."

Im get up, take im mat walk out for full view of dem all. Dem all amaze praise God, talk say, "We never see anytin like dis!"

Wetin di four friends show us about faith? Wetin you go ask Jesus to forgive — or to heal? And wetin e talk say im forgive di man first, den heal am?

If you dey read dis for Nigeria — or anywhere for di world where you read Pidgin — abeg, you fit take small time greet Jonathan?
