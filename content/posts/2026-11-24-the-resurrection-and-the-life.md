Lazarus don die four days. Im sisters Martha and Mary dey grieve. Martha meet Jesus for road and talk say, "Lord, if you bin dey here, my brother for no die."

Jesus talk say, "Your brother go rise again."

Martha talk say, "I know say im go rise again for di resurrection for di last day."

Jesus talk say, "I am di resurrection and di life. Anybody wey believe for me go live, even though dem die."

Den im go di tomb and call, "Lazarus, come out!" And Lazarus come out.

Im no only talk about resurrection. Im do am.

Wetin you think Martha hear for those words? If im be di resurrection, wetin death mean now? And wetin you go ask am for grave side?

*Di Bible words for here na dem translate am for dis site.*
