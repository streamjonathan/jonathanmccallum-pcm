*Di Transfiguration, Raphael (1518–1520), public domain.*

Jesus dey walk for di temple, for Solomon porch. Di leaders gather round am and talk say, "How long you go keep us for suspense? If you be di Christ, tell us plain."

Jesus answer, "I don tell una, but una no believe. Di works wey I dey do for my Father name testify about me."

Den im talk am plain: "I and di Father are one."

Di leaders pick up stones again. Dem talk say im dey blaspheme — dey make Imsef God. Jesus no take am back.

Im talk who im be, plain. And dem understand exactly wetin im mean.

Wetin you make of dat claim? If im and di Father na one, wetin dat change? And why you think dem reach for stones?
