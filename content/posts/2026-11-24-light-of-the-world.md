Jesus stand for temple, where dem light big lamps for di feast. Im tell di people, "I am di light of di world. Anybody wey follow me no go ever walk for darkness. Dem go get di light of life."

Di Pharisees tell am, "Na yousef you dey talk about. Dat one no count."

Jesus answer, "Even if I dey talk about mysef, wetin I talk na true. I know where I come from, and I know where I dey go."

Im no talk say Im fit point to di light. Im talk say Im be di light.

Wetin e mean to walk for darkness? Wetin go change if you follow di light instead? And where you think say Im dey lead you go?
