I dey spend plenty time dey listen to musicians for Instagram. Na some of dem I don dey listen to lately.

A good song and small joy fit lift a whole day. I think say na true. Song fit carry wetin lecture no fit carry. E fit hold hope, and sorrow, and faith, all inside three minutes. Wetin you don dey listen to lately?

Paul do something like dis for Athens. E use dia poetry and dia language, di way dem already understand God. E start from where people already dey. I think say music dey do dat one too. E meet people where dem dey. You don ever hear God inside song wey no be worship song?

No be all of dem dey sing worship songs. Most of dem just dey sing about life, and faith na part of am. Na dat one I like about dem. Tap one card to watch di song for Instagram.
