Jesus dey talk with di religious leaders for temple. Dem ask am who Im think say Im be. Dem don already know Im family. Dem know where Im come from. How Im fit talk like dis?

Jesus talk say, "Before Abraham don dey, I Am."

Abraham live two thousand years before Jesus. But Jesus no talk say, "I don dey before Abraham." Im talk say, "I Am." Na di words wey God talk to Moses for di burning bush: "I AM WEY I BE."

Di leaders understand wetin Im mean well well. Dem pick stone to throw am. But Jesus slip comot from dem.

Im no go back. Im no explain am comot. Im talk who Im be, clear clear.

Wetin you think Im mean? If Jesus na who Im talk say Im be, wetin e go change? And wetin you go do with Im words?
