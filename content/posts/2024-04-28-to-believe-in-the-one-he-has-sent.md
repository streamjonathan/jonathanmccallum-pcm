E get one time wey Jesus dey with im closest followers, and crowd of people just chop from am, like miracle. Dem wan know wetin e go do next. Instead to push dem comot, Jesus tell dem make dem find di food wey dey last forever. Di bread wey dey last forever. E dey talk about imself.

![Jonathan dey smile near di sea, with rocky shore for im back](/images/to-believe-in-the-one-he-has-sent.jpg)

Dat one don stay with me. Wetin e mean to believe for di One Wey God send? Belief na system of how to think everything through? Or e be sometin more like to show love to God and to oda people?

I think belief get plenty to do with di mind. But I see mountain bike park just there, and e make me think. Bicycle only carry you go somewhere when you put am for action. Belief be like dat too. E dey move when you live am.

And love na somehow di main part of all kinds of faith for Jesus. Love na di power behind dat belief. Jesus go on to talk say e na di bread wey give life. E na di water wey give life. If we chop and drink from am, we no go thirst again.

E no give us definition of wetin e mean to believe for am. E call imself di one wey start life and dey keep am going. No matter your background, or anywhere you dey read dis from, na di invitation be dat.
