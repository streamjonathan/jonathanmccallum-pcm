We all dey walk pass people for need. Most days we barely notice. Hello to evribodi wey dey read for Nigeria — I glad say you dey here.

Jesus tell dis story. E get rich man wey dress for purple and fine linen live for luxury every day. For im gate lay poor man name Lazarus, cover with sores, dey long to chop wetin fall from di rich man table. Even di dogs come lick im sores.

Both men die. Angels carry Lazarus go Abraham side. Dem bury di rich man, and for torment im look up see Abraham far away, with Lazarus by im side. "Papa Abraham, get mercy on me," im cry. "Send Lazarus make im dip tip of im finger for water cool my tongue, because I dey for agony for dis fire."

But Abraham talk, "Pikin, remember say for your lifetime you receive your good tins, while Lazarus receive bad tins. Now im dey comfort here and you dey for agony. And between us and you dem don set great chasm."

Di rich man beg Abraham make im send Lazarus go warn im five brodas. "Dem get Moses and di Prophets," Abraham answer. "Make dem listen to dem." "If pesin from di dead go to dem, dem go repent," di rich man talk. Abraham talk, "If dem no listen to Moses and di Prophets, dem no go convince even if pesin rise from di dead."

Who you dey walk pass for di gate? Wetin you dey listen to? And wetin go change if you really believe di warning?

If you dey read dis for Nigeria — or anywhere for di world where you read Pidgin — abeg, you fit take small time greet Jonathan?
